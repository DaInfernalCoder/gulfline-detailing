Vehicle preparation

What to flag before arrival

Loose belongings, child seats, delicate electronics, existing tears, dye instability, embedded pet hair, and the material of each seat or trim surface can affect access and scope. Customers should remove valuables and identify known problem areas before arrival.

Photos can help explain the condition, but they do not replace an on-site suitability check. Keep keys available and make sure the vehicle can be reached safely in the agreed appointment area.

Limits and exclusions

A detailing appointment is not mold remediation, biohazard treatment, mechanical repair, or a promise that every stain, odor, or permanent material change will be removed. Steam and shampoo methods depend on surface suitability and are confirmed before use.

No page on this site invents a guarantee, credential, technician count, operating hour, or fixed result. If the requested work falls outside the published menu, confirm another option before scheduling.

Questions before booking

Interior Car Detailing FAQs

Does interior detailing include every stain?

No. Stain response depends on the material, age, prior treatment, and depth of the mark. The vehicle is assessed before a result is represented.

Should I empty the vehicle first?

Remove valuables and as many personal items as practical. That improves access and keeps the requested scope clear.

Can steam cleaning be used everywhere?

No. Steam is considered only for suitable surfaces and conditions; scope is confirmed at the appointment.
